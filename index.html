Questions

What every owner asks first.

Is Rigo AI?

Yes. Rigo is an AI teammate who texts in your agency's name, in English and Spanish. Your team takes the calls, quotes and closes.

Does Rigo call my clients?

No. Rigo texts. When someone is ready, Rigo books them for a call with you or your team.

Do you sell leads?

No. Rigo only works with your own leads: people who already asked you for a quote.

How much work is this for me?

Very little. A short form and a spreadsheet from any system. We set everything up and write the messages. You approve them and take the calls.

Will my clients know it's AI?

If they ask, Rigo tells the truth. And the call is always with you or your team.

When do I pay?

Only when a trucker is booked on your calendar. Your first 3 calls are free. After that, you choose: pay per call or a monthly plan.

What's the catch?

There isn't one. If Rigo doesn't book you calls, you don't pay. Simple as that.
